# Privacy

HiddenSpace is designed to operate locally on the device and does not require a developer-operated backend service.

## Data collection

The public release is intended to include:

- no analytics SDK;
- no advertising SDK;
- no intentional telemetry or device tracking;
- no collection of UDID, IMEI, serial number, Apple ID, phone number, or contact data;
- no developer-operated user account system.

HiddenSpace configuration is stored locally for the tweak's own operation.

## Third-party repositories

If HiddenSpace is downloaded through a package repository such as Havoc, that repository may record download, account, payment, or operational information according to its own privacy policy. Those systems are separate from HiddenSpace itself.

## Changes

If a future release introduces any network service, analytics, crash reporting, or other data transmission, this privacy statement will be updated before that release is distributed.
